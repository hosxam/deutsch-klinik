# A1 Full Lesson Depth Audit

Scope: A1_lesson_3 through A1_lesson_17. The audit compared each lesson's visible fields in `germanLessons.json` with A1 grammar questions whose `taughtInLessonId` points to that lesson and A1 vocabulary entries whose `taughtInLessonId` or `lessonId` points to that lesson.

## A1_lesson_3
- Topic: Nouns, capitalization, gender, definite and indefinite articles.
- Linked question count: 22 grammar questions; 17 vocabulary entries.
- Gaps found: article strategy was too brief for der/die/das/ein/eine questions; noun capitalization and gender memorization needed clearer visible teaching.
- Expansions made: added detailed article explanation, nominative definite/indefinite tables, article-learning strategy, more examples, common mistakes, drills, pronunciation notes, and remediation.
- Remaining concerns: some vocabulary entries still carry legacy ASCII spellings outside the visible lesson body.

## A1_lesson_4

- Linked question count: 15 grammar questions; 25 vocabulary entries.

- Expansions made: added nominative-to-accusative table, accusative verb table, examples with den/einen/die/das, common mistakes, drills, pronunciation notes, and remediation.
