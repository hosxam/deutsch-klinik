# C1 Target Curriculum

## Philosophy

C1 is **advanced professional, academic, and nuanced German**. Learners can:
- Understand demanding, long texts and implicit meaning
- Express ideas fluently and spontaneously without much searching
- Use language flexibly and effectively for social, academic, and professional purposes
- Produce clear, well-structured, detailed text on complex subjects

At C1, the focus shifts from learning grammar structures to mastering stylistic nuance, register awareness, argumentation, and precision.

## Grammar Topics

1. **Nominal Style** — Advanced nominalization (Nominalstil)
2. **Sentence Transformation** — Converting between nominal and verbal style, restructuring
3. **Advanced Connectors** — je...desto, zwar...aber, nicht nur...sondern auch, geschweige denn
4. **Konjunktiv I** — Reported speech in formal/academic contexts
5. **Konjunktiv II** — Diplomatic nuance, hypotheticals, politeness
6. **Passive Alternatives** — sein+zu, lassen+sich, bekommen-Passiv, Passiversatz
7. **Participial Constructions** — Partizip I/II as adjectives, extended attributes
8. **Modal Verb Meanings** — Subjective use for speculation (dürfte, müsste, kann/könnte)
9. **Relative Clauses** — Extended relative clauses, relative clauses with prepositions
10. **Register Choice** — Formal vs informal variations, functional style
11. **Extended Infinitives** — um...zu, ohne...zu, anstatt...zu with complex control
12. **Complex Clause Order** — Multi-clause sentences, nested structures
13. **Propositional Adverbs** — da(r)-compounds, hier-compounds, advanced usage
14. **Word Order Variation** — Stylistic fronting, inversion for emphasis
15. **Nominalization** — Verb to noun conversion, functional verb structures

## Vocabulary Domains

- **Academic** — research, publishing, argumentation, methodology
- **Professional** — business, negotiations, contracts, presentations
- **Social** — nuanced opinions, media literacy, cultural references
- **Political** — policy analysis, political theory, governance
- **Healthcare** — medical terminology, health policy, ethics
- **Research** — scientific method, data analysis, paper structure
- **Germany-life** — civic participation, legal concepts, education system
- **Technology** — digital literacy, AI, media landscape
- **Environment** — sustainability, climate policy, ecological discourse
- **Cultural** — art, literature, philosophy, critical theory
